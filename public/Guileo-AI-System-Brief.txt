1. Executive Summary
Guileo AI is a multi-tenant SaaS platform that replaces the front desk with an AI voice agent
— and turns every interaction into customer intelligence the business can use.
Independent restaurants, dental and aesthetic clinics, and service businesses all share the same
operational pain: the phone never stops ringing, but they miss 30–40% of calls anyway. They
lose orders, appointments, and customers to competitors who happen to answer first. They also have
no idea who their callers are — there's no profile, no history, no follow-up, no marketing reach.
Guileo AI solves both halves of that problem at once. We provide a 24/7 multilingual AI agent that
answers calls, takes orders or schedules appointments, processes payments, and confirms by SMS and
WhatsApp — and behind it, a Customer Data Platform that turns every interaction into a unified
customer profile with auditable consent. From that data layer we power campaigns, ad audiences, and
predictive ML that drive repeat purchases and reduce churn.

4. Three Verticals, One Platform
Same backend, same consent layer, same intelligence products. Vertical-specific UX,
vocabulary, voice prompts, and integrations.
Strategic principle: A restaurant, a clinic, and a barbershop have nearly identical operational
needs — answer the phone, capture the customer, complete the transaction, follow up. The
differences are vocabulary and integrations, not architecture. Building once and adapting per
vertical is dramatically cheaper than building three separate companies. 
4.1 Guileo AI for Restaurants
The user: Independent restaurants and small chains, especially those losing margin to DoorDash/
UberEats and missing peak-hour calls.
Anchor tenant: New York Pão de Queijo, Astoria NY — bilingual EN/PT, 50+ menu items, breakfast
through dinner, delivery + pickup.
Specific features
Bilingual voice agent (English ↔ Portuguese auto-detect)
Menu management with three tiers: manual, AI-extracted from PDF/image, or POS-synced (T oast/
Square/Clover)
Kitchen ticket dispatch via email, thermal printer (HTTP), or tablet display
Delivery zone validation, NYC sales tax (8.875%), delivery fee logic
DoorDash Drive / UberDirect API integration for overflow delivery
Zero integration with consumer marketplaces (DoorDash/UberEats listings) — Guileo is the direct
channel
4.2 Guileo AI for Clinics
The user: Dental, dermatology, aesthetic, and small medical practices losing patients to no-shows
and missed after-hours calls.

5.4 Data Processing Agreement (tenant ↔ Guileo)
Each tenant signs a DPA at onboarding authorizing Tribeca Global Inc. (operating as Guileo AI) to
process their customer data for: (1) service delivery, (2) anonymized aggregate insights, (3) ML
training on de-identified data, (4) cross-tenant lookalike audience generation — but only when the
underlying customer also granted intelligence-tier consent. The two layers (tenant
authorization + customer consent) must both exist before any cross-tenant data use.

9. Appendix
Operational notes, terminology, and reference.
9.1 Glossary
Tenant A business (restaurant, clinic, salon) using Guileo AI as a customer.
Customer The end-user calling/messaging the tenant. Every customer belongs to exactly one
tenant.
CDP Customer Data Platform — the unified data layer storing customer profiles, orders,
events, and consents.
DPA Data Processing Agreement — legal contract between tenant and Guileo authorizing data
use.
DSR Data Subject Request — when a customer asks to view, export, correct, or delete their
data.
DSAR Data Subject Access Request — specifically the "show me everything you have on me"
request.
RLS Row-Level Security — Postgres feature enforcing tenant isolation at the database layer.
RFM Recency / Frequency / Monetary — classic customer segmentation framework.
LTV Lifetime Value — predicted total revenue from a customer over a time horizon.
BAA Business Associate Agreement — HIPAA-required contract when handling PHI.
PHI Protected Health Information — patient data subject to HIPAA.
9.2 Operating principles
Voice quality is non-negotiable. If the agent sounds robotic or stutters, no other feature
matters.
Multi-tenant from day one. Every table has a tenant_id. Every query filters by it. RLS is
enforced, not optional.
Consent is a feature, not paperwork. The privacy portal is part of the product, not the legal
department.
